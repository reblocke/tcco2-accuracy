# Future-setting disagreement in mmHg

TCCO2-008 uses the joint summary-data hierarchy in model.stan. Each prediction draws one setting exchangeable with the named source class and one paired occasion in that setting. Publication, analysis cohort and physical hospital have different meanings. Sample size informs precision. One cohort with documented disjoint patients may contribute alongside another from the same article.

The ledger is a derived overlay on Conway et al.'s [Figshare archive v2](https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.6244058.v2), CC BY 4.0, checked November 15, 2026. The checksum is in specification.json. The archive’s main object contains 75 rows. The 76-row union adds the ICU-only Bolliger cohort. Membership comes from the actual main, ICU, ARF and LFT objects. Kim and Hirabayashi report disjoint cohorts in the archive notes. Each stratum uses one selected summary per independent cohort. Distinct publications are treated as independent when curation finds no overlap signal. Identified unresolved overlap holds the affected fit. Pediatric/neonatal labels come from the respective source objects. Other ages are unspecified unless primary evidence supplies them. Maniscalco's LFT assignment includes adult respiratory inpatients.

Each row preserves the unstarred variance, limits and source-adjusted variance. All were checked against the source count ratio, repeated-measures formula, midpoint and width of the limits. The corrected legacy implementation uses the 76-row union and is preserved. For consistent archive summaries use the archived S2* exactly once as Conway's summary-level adjustment. Source-level adjustment remains an approximation to participant data. This disposition does not certify every originating paper's treatment of repeat measurements. The five conspicuous sign/width conflicts have original-report locators, corrected inputs where supported, and a hold where the evidence is insufficient. The published SD and baseline 35 observations take precedence over the mixed count of 55 for Maniscalco. See the decision column for the primary-report basis.

For m participants, the bias and corrected log-variance sampling variances are s²*/m and 2/(m−1). The observed log variance is ln(s²*)+1/(m−1). Absent a source covariance, a diagonal Gaussian sampling approximation is adopted. Estimated sampling covariances are treated as known. A supported dependent block specifies the full joint sampling covariance plus which summaries share latent setting effects. Each fit conditions only on the adjudicated rows for its declared reference class.

The independent normal and half-normal hyperpriors use SDs from specification.json. Mean bias centers at zero. The median setting SD centers at 4 mmHg. LKJ(2) regularizes correlation in sparsely observed strata. For half-normal log-variance heterogeneity of scale 0.6, E exp(τ²) is finite because 2×0.6²<1. The independent normal mean log variance has finite exponential moments. Positive-definite fixed sampling covariance bounds the Gaussian likelihood uniformly, so the posterior first and second moments of exp(μℓ+τℓ²/2) exist. Under unit conversion by c, scale both bias priors by c and shift mean log variance by 2 ln(c). Log-variance spread and correlation are dimensionless.

A fixed-seed pre-data simulation (600,000 draws, seed 20261115) gave a central disagreement interval of approximately −17.89 to 17.93 mmHg. Priors refer to mmHg and the natural logarithm of variance relative to 1 mmHg². The empirical PaCO₂ prior for inverse inference has separate cohort and support metadata.
