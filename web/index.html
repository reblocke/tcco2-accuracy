Population, assumptions and methods

The reference population comprises patients undergoing arterial blood gas testing in TriNetX. Each patient contributes equal weight across eligible draws on their first testing day within the selected setting. All settings uses the first eligible day overall. ICU is identified by inpatient critical-care billing. Ambulatory describes the encounter type.

Both thresholds apply to this population. Prediction assumes that its arterial distribution applies to this patient and that arterial minus transcutaneous disagreement follows the fitted pattern across PaCO₂ values. The overall studies include children and neonates. Acute respiratory failure studies supply the ED/inpatient fit and lung function studies supply the ambulatory fit.

The paired-measurement model uses evidence collected by Conway and colleagues. Results use the complete empirical distribution. Smooth curves show grouped probabilities, and relative likelihood is scaled to a peak of one. The initial chart spans the central 99.9% of posterior mass and both thresholds.

Research use. Independent biostatistical review and individual-patient coverage validation are pending.
