Source populations and methods

Conway reported 73 studies overall. The corrected legacy calculation uses 76 rows, including the ICU-only Bolliger cohort. The new overall fit uses 75 adjudicated units from 73 publications and incorporates the Simon steady-state correction. Each stratum was fitted separately with future_setting_joint_v1. Subgroup memberships overlap. Conway et al., Thorax 2019

The default predictions use the original TriNetX cohort over its full observation period. Each patient contributes total weight one, split equally across eligible arterial draws on the first testing day in the selected setting. All is selected independently across eligible settings. ICU is a critical-care billing proxy; ambulatory status does not establish pulmonary function testing. The summary lookup reports whole mmHg and whole percentages under the documented numerical reporting amendment. Exact priors and full curves remain private.

The synthetic example uses an illustrative PaCO₂ distribution on a 0.25 mmHg grid. The curves connect density estimates per mmHg. The interval uses the full grid. Each threshold percentage sums the posterior probabilities of all grid values at or above that threshold.
