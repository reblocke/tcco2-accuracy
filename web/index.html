Source populations and methods

Conway reported 73 studies overall. The corrected legacy calculation uses 76 rows, including the ICU-only Bolliger cohort. The new overall fit uses 75 adjudicated units from 73 publications and incorporates the Simon steady-state correction. Each stratum was fitted separately with future_setting_joint_v1. Subgroup memberships overlap. Conway et al., Thorax 2019
